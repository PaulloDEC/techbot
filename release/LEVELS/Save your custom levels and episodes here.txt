LEVELS - custom levels and episodes for Techbot
===============================================

Put custom levels (.LEV) and episodes (.EPI) here, whether you made them
in Shrapnel or downloaded them.

  * In Techbot, choose U)ser levels on the main menu to see and play them.
  * Each one keeps its own saved games and high scores, separate from the
    stock episodes.
  * Levels and episodes contain only their author's work.  The tiles,
    actors and sounds come from your own copy of the game, so a level
    built with Episode 2 or 3 graphics needs that episode in the game
    folder.

This folder must stay inside the game folder (the one with DN1.EXE).
